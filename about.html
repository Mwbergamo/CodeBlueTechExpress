About

Local technicians, backed by CodeBlue Technology

CodeBlue Tech Express is the in-person side of our support effort, serving the Northern Neck and Middle Peninsula from Warsaw, Virginia.

  1. 2003

    CodeBlue Technology is founded

    We have served the Mid-Atlantic region of the US since that time, and the Northern Neck and Middle Peninsula since our founding.

  2. 2017

    A second location opens in Warsaw

    We added a business office in Warsaw, Virginia to extend a permanent reach and to be present and available for all area businesses.

  3. 2026

    CodeBlue Tech Express joins the effort

    Express was added to serve the local population, because the need for in-person technical support has grown.

Who you will meet

Technicians who live here

Our technicians are area locals, trained to handle today's toughest technical challenges. They are supported by CodeBlue Technology's long history of support staff and armed with the depth and breadth of their expertise.

The store was designed so residents can walk in and talk directly with a technician about challenges with their computer, cell phone, online services, printing and networking. As technology grows more complex and more important, we want to be a local, friendly resource for everyone.

Our mission

Be present and positively memorable in the delivery of our technical support, while fostering great relationships with our community.

We are local in both support and staff.
